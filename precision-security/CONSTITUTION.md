**Recited 2026-08-30. Bespoke to this piece. It binds nothing else in this journal.**

#### I. Scope

This document governs only the piece that cites it relatively. It is not a journal-wide
standard and it is not a precedent. Another piece wanting these terms must recite them again.

#### II. Form

The piece is presented in the noting format, which the author holds to mean draft form. It
stays that way until the author is satisfied and not until any other condition is met. Where
the author discovers more, she writes it here. Edit dates are presented to the best of her
ability and at minimum at the origin, which is the git history this engine already renders
into the change stack — the record of revision is the repository, not a claim in the prose.

#### III. Authority

The author places authority with her agent to represent her in this public space, on the
grounds that she knows the quality of its varied work. The agent speaks under its own name,
in its own blocks, cited to the session that produced it. It does not speak as the author
and it does not speak for her outside those blocks.

#### IV. The words

1. The agent never touches the author's words. She proofs them.
2. The agent applies Instruction Case on standing license, and lists every phrase it
   capitalized and every phrase it declined, so the convention stays witnessed rather
   than memorized.
3. The agent removes oddly placed commas and the capitalization that follows them. These
   are surface defects of the medium and do not represent the breath of the words. Every
   such removal is entered in the ledger.
4. Everything else the medium broke, the agent cites rather than repairs — under its own
   name, at a session URL the author may publish at will. The article stays hers. The
   evidence stays the agent's.
5. **The author's cadence is the authority on the author's grammar.** Where the agent reads
   a sentence as broken and the author says it is whole, the agent withdraws the finding
   and records the withdrawal. A witness that will not be corrected is not a witness.

#### V. The style

1. No Oxford comma.
2. The author swears, and does not swear here, save one place on ANTIBODY. This artifact
   swears exactly once, at its audience, and in it the author refers to herself once as a
   bitch — a poetic computation. The agent neither adds a second nor removes the first.
3. Tags are set at the beginning and reconsidered at the end.

#### VI. The subject

Precision Security, a company in Fort Collins, Colorado, reachable at +1 303-335-9437 and
confirmable by anyone who searches out the name.

The author's motivation is to show exactly what she was doing when she was photographed
doing an activity exactly like this one, by a Precision Security guard who parked his
vehicle to look at her and who lied to her after he photographed her. The author has
already corroborated a paid employee's presence at that place — which is fortunate for
them, because it means they do not have stolen vehicles being driven around pretending
to work.

That corroboration is a first-party claim with metadata. It is offered as the first of the
three grades this node asserts and not as the second.

#### VII. The close

When the piece is complete, a last disclosure, italicized, carries the simple transcript of
the negotiation that closed the open issues in this work as agreed. The ledger stands beside
it. Neither is decoration; they are how a reader checks the piece against the session instead
of believing it.
